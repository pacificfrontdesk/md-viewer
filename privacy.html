Privacy Policy

MD Viewer for Chrome · last updated 24 September 2026

Who we are

MD Viewer is a Chrome extension published by Pacific Front Desk LLC, Los Angeles, California ("we", "us"), which is responsible for the extension and for this policy.

The short version

Your documents never leave your computer. MD Viewer has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and we never see what you read, write, copy or paste. The only personal data involved is what you give our payment provider if you choose to buy Pro.

What stays on your device

The extension saves the following in your browser's local extension storage, and nowhere else:

  • Your settings: theme, view mode and text size
  • The document you are currently editing (an autosaved draft)
  • Your Pro status and the count of free conversions used today

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

Pages, files and your clipboard

When you open a Markdown file, use smart paste, "Copy selection as Markdown" or "Convert page to Markdown", the content is processed entirely inside your browser. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. The extension only reads a page when you ask it to, by clicking the popup or a right-click menu item.

When you use "Open from URL", the extension asks your permission for that one website and then downloads the file directly from the address you entered.

Purchases and free trials

Pro is an optional purchase handled by ExtensionPay and Stripe. If you buy Pro or start a free trial, they process your email address and payment details under their own privacy policies. We receive only whether your account has paid and when your trial started, which the extension uses to unlock Pro features. We never see your card details.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or share data with anyone
  • We don't use data for advertising, profiling or credit decisions
  • We don't load remote code: everything the extension runs ships inside it

Children

MD Viewer is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before that version is published, and the change will be described here.
