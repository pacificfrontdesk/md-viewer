MD Viewer for Chrome

Local .md files don't render

Open chrome://extensions, click Details on MD Viewer and turn on Allow access to file URLs. Then reload the file.

Smart paste pasted plain text

Free accounts include 5 smart conversions a day; after that, paste falls back to plain text until tomorrow. Pro makes it unlimited. To paste plain text on purpose, press Ctrl + Shift + V.

I paid but Pro isn't unlocked

Open the MD Viewer popup and click Already paid? Restore, then sign in with the email you used at checkout.
